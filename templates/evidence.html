What is in a bundle? The findings (JSON and CSV), a timeline, the full audit-log hash chain, a manifest with the SHA-256 of every file, a chain-of-custody record, and a standalone verify_bundle.py checker. The manifest hash is also written into this installation's audit log, so a bundle edited after export can be recognised.
